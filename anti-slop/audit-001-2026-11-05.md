## Details per Finding

### Finding 1: Hardcoded fake stats & playtime in ProfilePage
- **Rule**: `R-17 (Hard Gate)` — FORBIDDEN: numbers and statistics without a real source. Empty is better than deceptive.
- **Location**: `src/components/ProfilePage.tsx`
- **Issue**: Displays hardcoded "14.6 hrs" and "4.2 hrs" playtime, and "4" badges on user profiles.
- **Fix**: Calculate playtime and badges from real game logs or show `0 hrs` / real count, without hardcoded fictitious numbers.

### Finding 2: Outdated authentication copy in HelpPage diagnostics
- **Rule**: `R-34 (Hard Gate)` — Anything presented as fact must be real and verifiable.
- **Location**: `src/components/HelpPage.tsx` (Line 161)
- **Issue**: Tells players to sign in with Google or Discord account.
- **Fix**: Update copy to: "Sign in with your email or username to verify your game entitlements."

### Finding 3: Assumed generic Unsplash person photo
- **Rule**: `R-23 / R-39 (Hard Gate)` — FORBIDDEN: assuming profile photos without explicit instruction.
- **Location**: `src/lib/authService.ts` & `src/components/ProfilePage.tsx`
- **Issue**: Hardcoded Unsplash stock photo of a random person as default avatar.
- **Fix**: Replace with an initial-based avatar (e.g. `KE` or first letter of Display Name) or a sleek geometric gaming gamer emblem.

### Finding 4: Missing keyboard focus rings
- **Rule**: `R-32 (Hard Gate)` — Interactive elements must be reachable with Tab and have visible focus states.
- **Location**: `src/components/StorePage.tsx` & `src/components/GameDetails.tsx`
- **Issue**: Interactive carousel arrows and download buttons have `focus:outline-none` without `focus-visible:ring-2`.
- **Fix**: Add `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#66c0f4] focus-visible:outline-none` to all interactive buttons.

### Finding 5: Brand identity & clone artifacts in Top Header Menu
- **Rule**: `Purpose-Gate (Identity & Originality)` — The product should feel unique with its own character, not an unedited clone.
- **Location**: `src/components/SteamHeader.tsx`
- **Issue**: Dropdowns literally say "Steam", "Sign In to Steam...", "Exit Steam", "Activate a Product on Steam...".
- **Fix**: Rebrand menu labels to "Kiraeve", "Sign In to Kiraeve...", "Exit Kiraeve Launcher", and "Activate a Product on Kiraeve...".

### Finding 6: Level badge fallback to Level 15
- **Rule**: `R-17 (Hard Gate)` — Display exact data or true defaults.
- **Location**: `src/components/ProfilePage.tsx` (Line 218)
- **Issue**: Fallback `user.level || 15` inflates player level.
- **Fix**: Change to `user.level || 1`.

### Finding 7: Uniform passive card glow
- **Rule**: `Purpose-Gate (Glow Everywhere)` — Technique without purpose must be refined.
- **Location**: `src/components/ProfilePage.tsx` (Lines 251-280)
- **Issue**: Passive stat cards have persistent glowing borders identical to primary active CTA cards.
- **Fix**: Reserve glow effects for active hover states or primary actions (Play / Download / Redeem).
